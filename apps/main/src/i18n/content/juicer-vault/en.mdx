You deposit one asset, for example ETH. It's supplied to the Hydration money market and you receive **vault shares**. Your exposure to the asset stays **1:1**; your deposit is never traded into the strategy.

The vault borrows HOLLAR against the pooled collateral and builds a looped **PRIME position**. PRIME earns yield from real-world credit. The position is built gradually, so new deposits start earning as they're deployed.

### Earnings

Harvested PRIME yield pays interest and fees first. The rest is converted into your deposited asset and added to your balance, with **nothing to claim**. Yield not yet harvested shows as **pending yield**. Earnings only arrive while the PRIME position is worth more than the vault borrowed, so they can be zero for a while.

### Withdrawals

Request all or part **at any time**. Your shares keep earning during the cooldown. After it, the amount you get back is fixed and your part of the position unwinds. Claim it **in parts** as it settles; anything recovered later can be claimed in HOLLAR.

### Risks

- A sudden drop in PRIME's price can liquidate the looped position.
- Withdrawals can take longer when many are open, and they can be paused.
- If the position falls short, your claim stays, but payment may wait for recovery funding.

Returns, principal and timing are not guaranteed.

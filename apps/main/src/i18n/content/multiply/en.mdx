### Correlated vs volatile loops

- **Correlated** loops pair assets that move together, like a staked token and its base, in the same E-mode category. They allow higher leverage and are rarely liquidated by price, so the main risk is borrow rates.
- **Volatile** loops pair assets that move independently. A price drop can liquidate them, so they need lower leverage and closer watching.

### Fees

When you open a position, you fund a small fee budget that pays for each automated step that builds, adjusts and unwinds it. Whatever is left is refunded when you close. Swaps also cost price impact, shown as entry and exit cost before you confirm.

### Liquidation

If the health factor drops below 1, part of the position is liquidated with a penalty. Positions don't deleverage automatically, so close if the liquidation price gets near.

### Borrow rates

You earn the gap between the deposit yield and the borrow rate. If borrowing costs more than the deposit earns, the position loses value.

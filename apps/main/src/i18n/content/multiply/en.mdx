### What is Multiply?

Multiply is leverage on a single asset. The position borrows against your collateral, swaps the loan for more collateral and supplies it, until it reaches your chosen leverage. It pays while the collateral earns more than the debt costs.

### Your position

Each position is its own contract, owned by whoever holds its NFT. It builds one swap at a time, so leverage climbs over a few minutes. Closing runs the swaps in reverse and pays out the rest. If a swap fails, the position pauses with your funds in it until you resume.

### Fees

Each swap pays a small fee from a budget you fund at opening. What's left comes back when you close.

### Risks

If the collateral falls against the borrowed asset and the health factor drops below 1, part of the position is liquidated. Higher leverage leaves less room, so check the liquidation price first.

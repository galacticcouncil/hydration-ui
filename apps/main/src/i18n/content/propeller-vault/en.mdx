A confirmed deposit supplies **your collateral asset** to the money market and mints funded vault shares. It does not borrow or swap during your deposit transaction. Keepers then borrow HOLLAR and build the shared **leveraged PRIME position** gradually through trades that respect the vault’s price limits.

Collateral awaiting deployment is pooled across the vault, including deposits and harvested earnings. It is not a separate personal queue or a promise of when your position will be fully deployed. Trading limits and market conditions determine the pace; the vault manages borrowing and rebalancing for you.

Harvested yield is converted into your collateral asset. Protocol fees and interest are paid before the remainder becomes funded collateral rewards. Keepers can then deploy that new collateral gradually; some earned yield stays invested in PRIME awaiting conversion. Returns vary with yield, borrowing rates and trading costs.

Converted earnings remain invested as **funded collateral shares**. Claiming adds those shares to your position, rather than paying out to your wallet; further strategy deployment happens gradually. Earnings awaiting conversion are an estimate, not a funded collateral balance.

### Key features

- The vault manages the leveraged position
- Available rewards compound into your deposited asset
- You can request a full or partial withdrawal when withdrawals are enabled

### Withdrawals and risk

Exits go through an **async withdrawal queue**. Your shares remain invested during the configured cooldown. When unwinding starts after that delay, the vault fixes your collateral claim. The cooldown is the earliest unwind start, **not a payout deadline**.

Your collateral becomes claimable as the withdrawal settles, **possibly in parts**. Later source recoveries may also become claimable in HOLLAR, even after you have claimed the collateral.

Juicer aims to preserve principal in the deposited asset. The leveraged PRIME position can lose value or be liquidated, and protection depends on functioning oracles, keepers and governance. A shortfall preserves your unpaid collateral claim but may require recovery funding before payment. This is not a guarantee of immediate withdrawal or full recovery.

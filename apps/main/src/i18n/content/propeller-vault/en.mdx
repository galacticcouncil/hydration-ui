Propeller supplies **your collateral asset** to the money market, borrows HOLLAR against it, and invests that HOLLAR in a shared **leveraged PRIME position**. The vault manages borrowing and rebalancing for you.

Harvested yield is converted into your collateral asset. Protocol fees and interest are paid before the remainder is compounded; some earned yield stays invested to cover execution costs. Returns vary with yield, borrowing rates and trading costs.

### Key features

- The vault manages the leveraged position
- Available rewards compound into your deposited asset
- You can request a full or partial withdrawal when withdrawals are enabled

### Withdrawals and risk

Exits go through an **async withdrawal queue**. Your shares remain invested during the configured cooldown. When unwinding starts after that delay, the vault fixes your collateral claim. The cooldown is the earliest unwind start, **not a payout deadline**.

Your collateral becomes claimable as the withdrawal settles, **possibly in parts**. Later source recoveries may also become claimable in HOLLAR, even after you have claimed the collateral.

Propeller aims to preserve principal in the deposited asset. The leveraged PRIME position can lose value or be liquidated, and protection depends on functioning oracles, keepers and governance. A shortfall preserves your unpaid collateral claim but may require recovery funding before payment. This is not a guarantee of immediate withdrawal or full recovery.

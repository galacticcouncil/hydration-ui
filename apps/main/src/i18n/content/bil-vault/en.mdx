### What is BIL?

BIL (**Brazilian Invoice Loans**) is a yield-bearing token built on **Decentral**, a fixed-rate lending protocol that finances Brazilian invoice receivables — short-term, real-world trade credit.

Lenders fund fixed-term Decentral positions, currently offering a fixed **18% APY over 60-day terms**. The BIL strategy pools these positions into a single liquid token that appreciates against **HOLLAR** as yield accrues and automatically reinvests matured positions to compound returns.

When you deposit HOLLAR, your strategy shares are supplied to a dedicated BIL money market in the same transaction. The token you receive and hold in your wallet is simply **BIL**.

### Withdrawals

You can exit in two ways:

- **Redemption queue:** Request a withdrawal and receive HOLLAR as the underlying Decentral positions mature. Your BIL continues earning yield until your request is settled.
- **Instant swap:** Swap BIL for HOLLAR through the **BIL/HOLLAR stableswap pool**, subject to available liquidity and price impact.

### Things to know

BIL returns are generated from the underlying invoice receivables and depend on their repayment. Withdrawals through the redemption queue may take up to approximately **62 days**. For a faster exit, you can swap through the BIL/HOLLAR pool, subject to available liquidity and price impact.
